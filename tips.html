💡 Snack Tips

Improve your snack game without becoming a professional chef.

⏱️ Tip #1 — Prepare Ahead

Keep simple ingredients ready in your kitchen so you can make something quickly when you're hungry or short on time.

💰 Tip #2 — Save Your Money

Choose affordable ingredients and buy things you can use in multiple recipes. Your wallet will thank you.

🧹 Tip #3 — Clean as You Go

Washing dishes while you cook means you won't be left with a pile of dirty plates after your snacking session.

🔥 Tip #4 — Don't Forget Safety

Be careful when using ovens, stoves, microwaves and sharp objects. If you're unsure how to use something, ask for help.

🧽 Tip #5 — Never Underestimate Nuggets

Nuggets can solve many problems. Hunger is one of them. Homework, unfortunately, is not.
