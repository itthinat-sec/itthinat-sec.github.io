👋 About Nuggetology

The science of snacking.

What is Nuggetology?

Nuggetology was originally meant to be a joke website about making a 1 kg nugget, but it transformed into a simple recipe website made for students.

Why Students?

I was a student too, and didn't have much time to make food. So I made this website to help other busy students find quick, easy and affordable snacks.

🧽 Our Mission

To help hungry students make snacks with less effort, less money and, of course, more nuggets.
