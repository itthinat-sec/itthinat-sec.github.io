🧽 The 1KG Nugget

⏱️ Cooking Time: Approximately 4 hours

🔥 Difficulty: Questionable

🛒 Ingredients

  • 1 kilogram of chicken nuggets or chicken mixture
  • Breadcrumbs
  • Eggs
  • Flour
  • Salt and pepper
  • Cooking oil

👨‍🍳 Instructions

  1. Prepare your chicken mixture and season it with salt and pepper.
  2. Shape the chicken mixture into one massive nugget.
  3. Coat the nugget with flour.
  4. Dip it into beaten eggs.
  5. Cover the entire nugget with breadcrumbs.
  6. Carefully cook the giant nugget until it is fully cooked.
  7. Let it cool down slightly before cutting and serving.
  8. Enjoy your absolutely gigantic nugget! 🍗
